    How it works

    Subdomains need separate approval. Reload pages after changes; close existing tabs when enabling blocking.

    PHP labels are historical observations, not vulnerability checks. Lists explain blocks; all unapproved hosts are blocked. Approving a host allows all its ports and HTTP, HTTPS and WebSocket requests.

    Custom imports replace the previous import and never change approvals. Everything stays in this browser profile.

    Disable or reload the extension on your browser’s Extensions page. Unpacked installs need manual updates.

    This does not patch servers. Already loaded content, existing connections, browser-protected traffic and service-worker cached responses may bypass blocking. Other profiles and apps are outside its scope.
